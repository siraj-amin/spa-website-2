The quiet power of scent in your evening routine

Why a familiar aroma can become the first signal that it is time to slow down.

Scent is closely tied to memory and mood. Choosing one gentle aroma for your evening can create a small, repeatable cue that says the day is winding down.

Choose what feels familiar

Try lavender, sandalwood or citrus, but let your own preference lead. The best scent is one that feels comforting rather than overpowering.
