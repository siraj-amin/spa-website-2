Making private self-care work around your schedule

Simple ways to turn a home or hotel visit into an unhurried, comfortable experience.

Self-care does not have to wait for a perfect day. A private appointment can fit around work, travel or a quiet evening at home when the setting is planned with intention.

Protect the time you set aside

Choose a window without back-to-back commitments, prepare a comfortable space and let the visit remain yours. A little breathing room makes the experience feel more restorative.
