Designing a mindful morning

A few intentional minutes can change the texture of your whole day.

Begin with one quiet choice: water, fresh air, stretching or simply leaving your phone untouched for a few minutes.

Start with what feels easy

Small rituals become lasting when they fit your real life. Let your morning be supportive, not another task list.
